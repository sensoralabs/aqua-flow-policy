Introduction

AquaFlow is a privacy-focused AI hydration coach designed to help users build healthy hydration habits.

Data We Store

  • Hydration logs and water intake history
  • Daily hydration goals
  • Reminder preferences
  • Name (optional)
  • Gender (optional)
  • Weight
  • Analytics and progress data
  • Achievements and milestones
  • App settings and preferences

AI Features

AquaFlow may generate hydration insights and recommendations using data stored on your device.

These recommendations are informational only and are not medical advice.

No Login & No Account

AquaFlow does not require registration, login, email address, phone number, or social accounts.

No Location Data

AquaFlow does not collect, store, or process location data and does not require location permissions.

Notifications

Hydration reminders are generated locally on your device.

CSV & PDF Export

Exported reports are generated locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Data Sharing

AquaFlow does not sell, share, rent, or transfer user data to third parties.

Data Storage

All hydration data, analytics, profile information, and settings remain stored locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

AquaFlow does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
