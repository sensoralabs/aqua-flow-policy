Introduction

AquaFlow is a privacy-focused AI hydration coach designed to help users build healthy hydration habits. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data.

Data We Store

  • Hydration logs and water intake history
  • Daily hydration goals
  • Reminder preferences
  • User profile information (name, gender, weight)
  • Hydration analytics and progress data
  • Achievements, streaks and milestones
  • Application settings and preferences

All data is stored locally on your device.

AI Features

AquaFlow includes AI-powered hydration coaching features that analyze hydration history, goals, streaks and usage patterns stored on your device.

AI-generated recommendations are intended for informational purposes only and should not be considered medical advice.

No Login & No Account

AquaFlow does not require account creation, registration, email addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or social logins.

No Location Data

AquaFlow does not access, collect, store, or process location data. The application does not require location permissions.

Notifications

AquaFlow uses local notifications to provide hydration reminders and smart reminder schedules selected by the user.

Notifications can be modified or disabled at any time from the app settings.

CSV & PDF Export

Users may export hydration data as CSV or PDF files.

Exported reports are generated locally on the device and are never uploaded to AquaFlow servers.

Data Sharing

AquaFlow does not sell, rent, transfer, or share user data with third parties.

AquaFlow does not use user data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

Data Storage

Hydration logs, profile information, analytics, achievements, settings, and reports are stored locally on your device.

AquaFlow does not maintain cloud accounts or remote user profiles.

Children's Privacy

AquaFlow is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided by a child, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of AquaFlow after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
