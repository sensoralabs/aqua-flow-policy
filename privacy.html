Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions

Introduction

Aqua Flow is a hydration reminder and water tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting user data. The app does not require user login or account creation.

Data We Store

  • Water intake history
  • Daily hydration goals
  • Reminder preferences
  • App settings

All data is stored locally on your device only. Aqua Flow does not collect or store personal identifiers.

No Login & No Account

Aqua Flow does not require user registration, login, email address, phone number, or any personal account information.

Location Permission (Optional)

Location access is optional and used only to provide weather-based hydration insights. Location data is not stored, saved, or shared.

Notifications

Aqua Flow uses local notifications to remind users about hydration based on selected schedules. Notifications can be disabled at any time.

CSV & PDF Export

Users can export hydration data as CSV or PDF files. These files are generated locally on the device and are never uploaded to any server.

Data Sharing

Aqua Flow does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. No data is used for advertising or analytics purposes.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the app means acceptance of the updated policy.
